# The four hard entry checks — expanded

Load this file when the user is serious about market entry (has budget, timeline or an existing business).

## Check 1 — Trust
Ask:
- Who does this customer already trust with money? (bank, church, family, cooperative, platform, agent)
- Is trust transferred person-to-person or verifiable by institution?
- What happens socially when a seller disappoints a buyer here?
Design rule: route through existing trust, or manufacture it visibly (escrow, refund guarantee,
named human contact, physical address, certification).
Failure pattern: assuming your brand is trusted because it is trusted elsewhere.
Evidence in corpus: `ebay-china-2003-2006`, `m-pesa-2007-now`, `grameen-bank-1983`, `taobao-alibaba-2003`.

## Check 2 — Payment rail
Ask:
- What share of the target segment has a card, a bank account, a mobile money wallet?
- Is instalment / buy-now-pay-later expected rather than optional?
- Does the seller need to collect cash physically to control risk?
Design rule: build for the rail that exists today, not the one you wish existed.
Failure pattern: card-only checkout in a cash economy; subscriptions in irregular-income segments.
Evidence in corpus: `afterpay-2014`, `m-pesa-2007-now`, `dominos-india-1996`.

## Check 3 — Decision unit
Ask:
- Is the buying decision made by one person, a couple, a family, a group, a works council, a government body?
- Does the decision need consensus (nemawashi-style consensus-building, eldercare input, tribal/family approval)?
- Who loses status if the purchase fails?
Design rule: sell to the decision unit, and give the advocate inside it ammunition.
Failure pattern: B2C messaging aimed at an individual in a household decision.

## Check 4 — Legitimacy and permission
Ask:
- Which licences, permits, certifications and inspections are required? What is the realistic timeline?
- Any religious, dietary, or cultural constraints on the product or its marketing?
- Labour law: works councils, union norms, minimum wage, dismissal rules.
- Foreign ownership limits, local partner requirements, currency controls.
Design rule: treat compliance cost and timeline as part of the product design, not an admin afterthought.
Failure pattern: launching then discovering you need certification you cannot get for 9 months.
Evidence in corpus: `walmart-germany-1997-2006`, `coca-cola-india-1977-1993`, `crikey-china-local-media`.

## Scoring (optional, for comparing two markets)
Rate each check 0–3 (0 = blocked, 3 = easy). Sum. Anything under 5/12 total means a redesign,
not a launch plan. Never present the score without the four narratives.
